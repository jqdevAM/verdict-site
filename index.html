Verdict

Google Play review results, on your phone.

You send a release to Google Play and wait for Google's review. Verdict waits for you: sign in with the Google account you use for the Play Console, and it tells you the moment a release is approved, rejected or published — without opening the console to check.

What it does

  • Every release, with its review state Per app and per track: draft, in review, approved, not approved, published.
  • A notification when Google answers Approved, not approved or published — and nothing for the changes you made yourself a second ago. Google pushes nothing, so the app checks, as often as every fifteen minutes.
  • Reviews, answered in two taps Canned replies kept on the phone: pick one, edit it, send.
  • Crash and ANR rates Each app's daily figures from Android vitals.

What it does not do

No account with us, no server of ours, no analytics, no ads. The app talks to Google's own APIs with the permission you give at sign-in, and keeps what it needs on your phone. The only thing it ever changes in your Play Console is a reply to a review that you wrote and sent. Signing out revokes its access.
